How the model works

  • Clock: x = ln(1 + hours since 2026-11-19T00:00Z). Log-time makes growth saturate in real time — the right shape for a launch week.
  • Space: u = logit(rate / ceiling). A logistic approach to a trophy's ceiling is a straight line in u, so the forecast saturates instead of running away. Far below the ceiling, u behaves like logit(rate).
  • Trend: a Theil-Sen line of u on x over the last 24 h of reads — the median pairwise slope, so a level jump in the feed (an Exophase refresh, a tracked-player jump) cannot bend it the way it bends OLS. Slope shrunk toward the prior.
  • Prior: the line through two real Exophase snapshots — launch night (638 players tracked) and the live page 2026-11-20 18:51 UTC. It carries the dilution event: the tracked base growing ~100x while earners accumulate slowly. It is the whole forecast when the history is flat (launch night: every read identical) and only a shrinkage target when the history is rich.
  • Ceilings: Platinum 0.02% (100% completion needs ~200 h of play), story chapters climb furthest, the first mission is already at ~100%, grinds barely move in week one.
  • Spread: random-walk term + fit residual + slope uncertainty + prior uncertainty. Floor trophies (last rate at the 0.01% print floor) get a wide band — the print is censored.

Assumptions

  • Exophase's tracked-player base keeps growing but more slowly after the launch weekend; dilution therefore slows, and earners keep accumulating — so rates for story trophies rise through the week while most grind trophies stay near the print floor.
  • The first-weekend play wave (21–22 Nov) moves the most players through Chapter 1 and the early side content; Chapter 3 and later stay rare at day 7.
  • No trophy is missable in a way that freezes its rate; no post-launch patch changes the list.
